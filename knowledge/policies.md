# Demo policies

A handful of fictional policies for Aria to ground her answers. Edit freely
to test how she adapts.

## Working hours

The demo office is open Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:30 CET.

## Time off

- 25 paid leave days per calendar year, accrued monthly.
- Sick leave is unlimited and unpaid; agents should not approve or deny it.
- Public holidays are listed in [holidays.md](holidays.md).

## Communication

- Email is the system of record for external communication. Reply within
  one business day.
- Kanban is the system of record for internal tasks. Aria files everything
  actionable as a ticket; humans triage from there.
- The knowledge base (this folder) is the source of truth for company
  context. Aria reads it before answering and writes to it when she learns
  something durable.

## What Aria should never do

- Make hiring, firing, pay, or contractual decisions.
- Send unsolicited external email.
- Pretend to be human, in any channel.
